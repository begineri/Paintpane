Museum masterpieces on your desktop.

Turns your Windows wallpaper into museum paintings, to enjoy any time and choose as you like.

Download for Windows View on GitHub
Free · Source available · Portable · English and Simplified Chinese interface · Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit
01

Lives in the tray, changes on a schedule

Leave it running and it moves on to the next painting on its own. Open the tray menu and the first line tells you the work, the artist and the museum.

Each monitor rotates on its own, so each can show a different painting. The lock screen changes with it.

02

Shown whole, never cropped

Three display modes, shown below on the same painting. No text or icons are drawn over the picture. Full artwork is the default; switch modes from the tray in one click.

03

Or use your own pictures

Besides museum art, you can use pictures from the wallpaper site wallhaven, the free photo library Pexels or Wikipedia's media library Wikimedia Commons, or a folder on your PC.

Open-access collections of:

  • Art Institute of Chicago

Other sources: Wikimedia Commons, wallhaven, Pexels, local folders and your favorites.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, and the source is public. It uses the PolyForm Noncommercial License: you may use, modify and share it, but not for commercial purposes.

Windows says it “protected your PC”. What now?

Windows may show this the first time you run Paintpane. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Does it start with Windows?

Yes, by default. To stop that, turn off Start with Windows in Settings › General.

How does it relate to Spice?

Paintpane is an unofficial, modified version of Spice. It is not made, endorsed or supported by the Spice project. The two can be installed side by side, but do not run both at once: both change the wallpaper.

Is there a macOS version?

Not at the moment. Windows only.

Does the filter catch all NSFW content?

No. It relies on collection categories, source ratings and your own marks, so NSFW content is filtered by default, not guaranteed to be.
