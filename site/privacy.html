Privacy

Applies to Paintpane for Windows: the portable exe from this site and the Microsoft Store version. Last updated 19 November 2026.

In short

Paintpane does not collect any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Nothing about you or your PC is sent to the developer.

It downloads pictures from the sources you turn on, and keeps your settings and pictures on your PC.

What is kept on your PC

None of this leaves your PC, except an API key, which is sent only to the site it belongs to.

What it connects to

Paintpane connects directly to these servers. It sends no information about you; like any website, they see your IP address and which pictures are requested, and their own privacy policies apply.

  • Museum open-access servers, for the museums you turn on: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Art Institute of Chicago, Cleveland Museum of Art, Rijksmuseum, National Palace Museum (Taipei), Statens Museum for Kunst, J. Paul Getty Museum, National Gallery of Art (Washington), Yale University Art Collections and Paris Musées, and the image servers they use.
  • Wikimedia Commons, if you turn it on.
  • wallhaven, if you turn it on. If you enter your wallhaven API key, it is sent to wallhaven with each request.
  • Pexels, if you turn it on. Pexels needs an API key, which is sent only to Pexels.
  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), for updated lists of museum collections.
  • GitHub (api.github.com), to check for a new version. Portable exe only; the Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store and does not check.

Links you click in the app, such as a work’s page at its museum or a museum on a map, open in your web browser.

Paintpane also runs a small local service on 127.0.0.1 (port 49452) for its own pages. It cannot be reached from other computers and refuses requests from websites.

Removing your data

  • Microsoft Store version: uninstall it in Settings › Apps. Windows deletes the app’s private folder. These stay and can be deleted by hand: %APPDATA%\fyne\PaintpaneStore (the settings file), Pictures\Paintpane and Documents\Paintpane, and an API key you entered, in Control Panel › Credential Manager › Windows Credentials.
  • Portable exe: turn off Start with Windows in Settings › General, quit the app, then delete the exe, %LOCALAPPDATA%\Paintpane and %APPDATA%\fyne\Paintpane, and an API key you entered in Credential Manager.

To delete only the downloaded pictures, use Clear cache in Settings › Advanced › Storage.

Children

Paintpane collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If what Paintpane stores or connects to changes, this page is updated in the same release, and the date at the top changes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
